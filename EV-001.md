## Definition of done

EV-001 completes when both are recorded:

1. **Independent reproduction by a non-maintainer**
   - verifies the archive SHA-256 and all 581 manifest digests;
   - runs the 606 tests on a clean environment;
   - records OS, Python, exact command, pass/fail and snapshot.
2. **Independent adversarial review**
   - a reviewer-designed conflict case, counterexample, or capability-boundary
     analysis;
   - a negative result showing the protocol cannot express part of the case is a
     valid completion.

Maintainer comments, GitHub-hosted CI, and same-host container runs do not count
as independent evidence.

## Intake rules

- Preserve the frozen tag/archive; do not edit RC files to fit a review.
- Negative or gap-finding reports are valid evidence.
- Reproducible contract defect → issue + minimal fixture; ambiguity →
  clarification proposal; expressiveness gap → protocol revision proposal.
- A protocol change requires a new candidate version; it does not mutate this RC.

Use `REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md` and `scripts/verify_snapshot.py`.
